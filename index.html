Mn-IQAI-driven manganese intelligence & production-risk platform

Mn-IQ observes manganese mine surface activity from space, compares it with available reported production, and ranks areas for further exploration. It flags and explains; a person decides.

Observe

Mine monitoring. Pit, waste-dump, stockpile and radar change over one opencast mine, including monsoon months.

Verify

Production-risk monitoring. Observed activity and publicly reported production as two independent streams. Divergence goes to a person for review.

Prioritise

Exploration prioritisation. Satellite, terrain, geology and known occurrences combined into relative priority, always shown with uncertainty.

The story in nine steps

  1. A real manganese mine
  2. Observed from space
  3. Surface change derived

  5. Divergence or risk flagged

  7. Uncertainty shown

  9. A person reviews and decides

Why grade matters

A mine can meet its tonnage target and still fall short on ore that meets a buyer's grade. Mn-IQ frames production in grade-qualified tonnes, shown as an illustrative scenario because grade data is not public.

Validation centre

Six tests decide whether Mn-IQ's claims hold. Each status shows the real state of the work. No metric appears here until a test has produced it.

B · Blind deposit test

Leave-one-out over every known deposit, with a spatial buffer so nearby samples cannot leak the answer.

C · ML vs simple baseline

The same blind test on a band-ratio / weighted-overlay baseline, on identical folds. ML is not assumed to win.

D · Sentinel-1 monsoon test

Usable Sentinel-2 scenes against Sentinel-1 scenes per month over the mine. Radar complements optical under cloud; it does not make monitoring immune to every monsoon condition.
